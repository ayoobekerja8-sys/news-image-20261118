GULF • CONFLICT WIDENS

Gulf war widens as Iran hits Kuwait and Bahrain

Early Wednesday, Iran fired drones at US installations in Kuwait and Bahrain. Bahrain said it intercepted the attacks; neither country reported casualties or damage. The strikes follow US jets hitting a missile launch site on Qeshm Island on Tuesday. Iran also downed a US MQ-9 near the Strait of Hormuz; Yemen’s Houthis said they targeted Saudi pipeline pumping stations.

Kerch Bridge hit again

Ukrainian drones and missiles struck for a second day. A road span collapsed into the strait; traffic halted. Russian missiles hit two Kyiv bridges, cutting water and heat.

Three powers meet

Xi and Putin opened APEC in Shenzhen. Trump arrived Wednesday; a three-way meeting is set for Thursday. Xi opposed trade protectionism; Putin criticised Western sanctions.

Senate flips

Mary Peltola defeated Dan Sullivan in Alaska. Democrats take a 56–44 majority, their largest since 2009 and ninth Senate flip of the midterms.

GULF — CONFLICT WIDENS

Gulf war widens as Iran hits Kuwait and Bahrain

Early Wednesday, Iran fired drones at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain; both were intercepted, with no damage or casualties. It was the third attack on Bahrain in five days, in the war's ninth month. The strikes followed US jets hitting a missile site on Qeshm Island on Tuesday; Houthis said they hit Saudi pipeline pumps.

Kerch Bridge hit a third time

Third strike in three days on Wednesday; traffic halted again. On Tuesday a 227-metre road arch fell into the strait. In Odesa, 4 killed, 17 injured.
